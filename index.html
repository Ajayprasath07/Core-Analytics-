Introduction

The Core of Every Political Decision

Political campaigns are shaped by people, perceptions, issues and data. At CORE Analytics, we bring these elements together to create a clear understanding of the political landscape.

From constituency-level research to campaign strategy and digital communication, we provide the intelligence and strategic support required to navigate a complex political environment.

Our story

Built Around the Core of Political Intelligence

CORE Analytics is a political consultancy focused on understanding the realities that shape elections and public opinion. We combine political consulting, data analytics, research, campaign strategy and digital intelligence to provide a comprehensive view of the political landscape.

Our work is grounded in one belief: better information leads to better decisions.

We study the numbers, understand the people behind them, identify emerging trends, and translate insights into practical strategies — whether that means understanding a constituency, identifying key issues, developing a campaign strategy or shaping digital communication.

Our Vision

To bring greater clarity and intelligence to political decision-making.

Our Mission

To transform political data and ground-level insights into actionable strategies that create measurable impact.

04 / Service

Constituency Research

Understanding every constituency at the ground level

Every constituency has its own political dynamics. We conduct detailed constituency-level research to understand the people, issues, voting patterns, political preferences, local dynamics and factors influencing electoral behaviour.

Questions our research answers

  • Who are the key voter groups?
  • What issues matter most?
  • How is voter sentiment changing?
  • What are the strengths and vulnerabilities within the constituency?
  • What local factors could influence electoral outcomes?

Our research combines available data with structured field insights to create a comprehensive picture of the ground reality.

Because political strategy begins with understanding the ground.

Our approach

Research. Analyse. Strategise. Execute.

Our approach is built around four interconnected stages. Each one feeds the next, and the cycle repeats as the political environment changes.

01 — Research

Gather the ground truth

We gather information from electoral data, surveys, constituency research, public sentiment, digital conversations and other relevant sources.

02 — Analyse

Find what the data means

We identify patterns, trends, voter segments, key issues, opportunities and risks within the available information.

03 — Strategise

Turn insight into direction

We translate insights into clear political and campaign strategies aligned with specific objectives.

04 — Execute

Support the work on the ground

We support implementation through campaign planning, communication, digital strategy, monitoring and continuous optimisation.
